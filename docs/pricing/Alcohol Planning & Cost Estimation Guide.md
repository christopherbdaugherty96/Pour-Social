# Pour Social Alcohol Planning & Cost Estimation Guide

This guide supports client education, quote review, and Nova-generated planning drafts.

Canonical assumptions live in:

`docs/automation/ALCOHOL_PLANNING_ASSUMPTIONS.md`

## Standard bottle planning rule

Pour Social uses:

```text
1 bottle / 750ml liquor = 14 cocktails
```

A 750ml bottle contains about 25.36 oz. At a theoretical 1.5 oz pour, that can produce about 16 cocktails, but Pour Social uses 14 for safer event planning.

This accounts for:

- overpours
- spillage
- batching loss
- recipe variation
- event-speed inconsistency

## Drink estimate formula

```text
estimated_drinks = drinking_guests × service_hours × 0.95
```

Example:

```text
100 drinking guests × 4 hours × 0.95 = 380 estimated drinks
```

## Cost-per-cocktail planning

Formula:

```text
cost_per_cocktail = bottle_price ÷ 14
```

Example:

```text
$28 bottle ÷ 14 = $2.00 per cocktail
```

## Client message

Use this explanation with clients:

> We plan using 14 cocktails per 750ml liquor bottle to protect against overpours, spillage, and event-speed variation. It is a safer planning number than the theoretical bottle yield.

## Important policy note

Pour Social does not sell alcohol. Clients purchase alcohol themselves or pre-order it from a retailer.

Pour Social may provide planning estimates and shopping lists, but the client remains responsible for final purchase quantities.
