POUR SOCIAL
Alcohol Shopping Calculator
Client Planning Tool

Purpose

The Pour Social Alcohol Calculator helps clients estimate how much alcohol they should purchase for their event.

Pour Social provides:

• staffing guidance
• mixers
• soda
• juices
• fruit and garnishes
• syrups
• bar tools and equipment
• event bar setup and service coordination

Clients purchase or pre-order alcohol. Pour Social does not sell alcohol.

---

Canonical Planning Assumptions

This guide follows the canonical assumptions in:

docs/automation/ALCOHOL_PLANNING_ASSUMPTIONS.md

Core assumptions:

• 0.95 drinks per drinking guest per service hour
• 14 cocktails per 750ml liquor bottle
• 5 glasses per wine bottle
• 24 beers per case
• 1–1.5 lb of ice per guest

Formula:

Estimated drinks = drinking guests × service hours × 0.95

Example:

100 drinking guests
4 hours of service

100 × 4 × 0.95 = 380 estimated drinks

• Beer: 20%
• Wine: 20%
• Cocktails: 60%

---

Bottle Conversions

Liquor Bottle (750ml)

Planning yield: 14 cocktails per bottle.

This is a safe planning yield that accounts for overpours, spillage, recipe variance, batching loss, and event-speed inconsistency.

Wine Bottle

Planning yield: 5 glasses per bottle.

Beer Case

Planning yield: 24 beers per case.

---

Important Client Note

This is a planning estimate only. Clients provide alcohol and remain responsible for final purchase quantities.

Pour Social helps estimate quantities based on guest count, service time, and menu style, but actual consumption may vary.

---

Alcohol Purchase Options

Option 1 — Client Supplies Alcohol

Client purchases and brings all alcohol to the event.

Option 2 — Client Pre-Orders Alcohol

Client may pre-order alcohol from a retailer.

Pour Social may pick up the order 1–3 days before the event only if the order has been fully paid for by the client.

Pour Social only transports alcohol and does not purchase or sell alcohol.

---

Helpful Tip

It is usually safer to purchase slightly above the estimate than to run out during service, especially for weddings, outdoor events, and long receptions.
