POUR SOCIAL
Alcohol Shopping List Guide

Purpose

This guide helps clients estimate how much alcohol to purchase for an event based on drinking guest count, service duration, and drink menu style.

Pour Social does not sell alcohol. Clients purchase alcohol themselves or pre-order it from a retailer.

This guide follows the canonical assumptions in:

docs/automation/ALCOHOL_PLANNING_ASSUMPTIONS.md

---

General Rule

Planning drinks = drinking guests × service hours × 0.95

Example:

100 drinking guests
4 hour event

100 × 4 × 0.95 = 380 estimated drinks

---

Bottle and Case Conversions

• 750ml liquor bottle = 14 cocktails for planning
• Wine bottle = 5 glasses
• Beer case = 24 beers
• Ice = 1–1.5 lb per guest

---

Drink Type Breakdown

Balanced Event

• Beer: 40%
• Wine: 30%
• Cocktails: 30%

Beer & Wine Only

• Beer: 55%
• Wine: 45%
• Cocktails: 0%

Cocktail-Focused Event

• Beer: 20%
• Wine: 20%
• Cocktails: 60%

---

Balanced Event Quantity Guide

50 Drinking Guests (4 hours)

Estimated drinks:

50 × 4 × 0.95 = 190 drinks

Recommended alcohol:

• Beer: 76 drinks → 4 cases
• Wine: 57 glasses → 12 bottles
• Cocktails: 57 cocktails → 5 liquor bottles

---

100 Drinking Guests (4 hours)

Estimated drinks:

100 × 4 × 0.95 = 380 drinks

Recommended alcohol:

• Beer: 152 drinks → 7 cases
• Wine: 114 glasses → 23 bottles
• Cocktails: 114 cocktails → 9 liquor bottles

---

150 Drinking Guests (4 hours)

Estimated drinks:

150 × 4 × 0.95 = 570 drinks

Recommended alcohol:

• Beer: 228 drinks → 10 cases
• Wine: 171 glasses → 35 bottles
• Cocktails: 171 cocktails → 13 liquor bottles

---

Mixer Quantities

Mixer needs depend on the final drink menu. For most balanced events, start with:

For 100 drinking guests:

• Club soda — 36–48 cans
• Tonic water — 18–24 cans
• Ginger beer — 12–24 bottles
• Cranberry juice — 2–3 large bottles
• Pineapple juice — 1–2 large bottles
• Orange juice — 1–2 large bottles
• Lime juice — 1 large bottle
• Simple syrup — 1 bottle

---

Garnishes

For 100 drinking guests:

• Limes — 30–40
• Lemons — 15–25
• Oranges — 6–10
• Mint — 2–4 bunches
• Cherries or olives — optional, menu-dependent

---

Ice

Plan for approximately:

1–1.5 lb of ice per guest.

Example:

100 guests → 100–150 lb ice

---

Event Planning Tip

Limiting cocktail menus to 2–3 signature drinks plus beer, wine, and simple highballs improves service speed and reduces waste.

Pour Social can adjust alcohol quantities based on the final drink menu.

---

Alcohol Pickup Option

Clients may pre-order alcohol from a retailer and request Pour Social pickup 1–3 days before the event.

Orders must be fully paid by the client before pickup.

Pour Social only transports alcohol and does not purchase or sell alcohol.

---

Client Disclaimer

This is a planning estimate only. Clients provide alcohol and remain responsible for final purchase quantities. Actual consumption may vary by guest behavior, event length, menu style, weather, and venue rules.
